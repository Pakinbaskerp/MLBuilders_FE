Our Story

ML Builders began with a simple frustration: too many families were promised dream homes and handed delays, hidden costs, and shortcuts instead. Our founders, both from civil engineering backgrounds, set out to do it differently — one project at a time, with a small crew that cared as much about the finish as the foundation.

Today, that same philosophy has grown into a full-service construction and interior design company serving residential and commercial clients across Tamil Nadu — but the core hasn't changed: transparent communication, skilled hands, and a promise we keep.
